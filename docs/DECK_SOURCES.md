## Problem: the scale of scams

| Fact | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Malaysia recorded **66,204 online fraud cases in 2025, with RM 2.97 billion lost**, up from 35,368 cases and RM 1.57 billion in 2024 | Bernama, 15 Jun 2026, quoting the IGP: <https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2568694> (also NST 16 Jun 2026; Home Ministry written reply, Jun 2026) | NEWS ONLY |
| Official alternative: the reported impact of scams was **RM 2.8 billion in 2025** (PDRM data) | BNM Annual Report 2025, Box 4 (31 Mar 2026): <https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/ar2025/box4> | VERIFIED |
| ⭐ Malaysians aged **61+ were 8.9% of online-fraud victims (Jan–Aug 2024) but lost 27.9% of the money (RM 255.4 million)**; for 2021–2023 they were 6.4% of victims and 20% of losses (RM 552.5 million) | Bernama, 28 Sep 2024, PDRM CCID director: <https://bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2345600> | NEWS ONLY |
| ⭐ **About 95% of online fraud cases in Malaysia are "authorised" transactions**, where the victim approves the payment herself | BNM Annual Report 2025, Box 4: <https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/ar2025/box4> | VERIFIED |

## Why today's controls miss her

| Fact | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| In **September 2022** BNM required banks to move off SMS OTP, tighten fraud detection, add a cooling-off period for first-time enrolment, restrict authentication to one device, and set up dedicated scam hotlines | BNM Governor's speech and press release, 26 Sep 2022: <https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/financial-crime-exhibition-speech-en> | VERIFIED |
| A customer **"kill switch"** was added in 2023; BNM reported a **58% fall in unauthorised online banking transactions** afterwards | BNM Governor's speech, 3 Oct 2023: <https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/g-spch-ifctf23> | VERIFIED |
| Banks blocked about **RM 1.2 billion** of fraudulent transactions in 2025 (RM 399 million in 2024), helped by the National Fraud Portal launched in 2024 by BNM, PayNet and the banks | BNM Financial Stability Review 2H 2025: <https://www.bnm.gov.my/publications/fsr2025h2/ch1c> | VERIFIED |
| ⭐ BNM's compensation policy for e-banking fraud (SEFT, in force 1 Oct 2024) **excludes payments the victim "willingly performed and approved"**, such as love, investment and parcel scams | SEFT policy document, para 5.2: <https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/pd-fair-treatment24> | VERIFIED |
| BNM is **studying whether to widen SEFT** to protect vulnerable consumers | BNM Annual Report 2025, Box 4 | VERIFIED |

**The point for slide 3:** banks have strong controls against *outsiders* taking over an account, and compensation for *unauthorised* payments. The ~95% of scams where the customer pays the scammer herself fall outside both. Teman works at that moment.

## Regulation the product lines up with

| Fact | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ BNM's **RMiT** policy (revised 28 Nov 2025, reissued 25 Sep 2026) requires that **"higher risk scoring must be applied for vulnerable customers (e.g. senior citizens, … customers with lower literacy on the safety of electronic banking …)"** | RMiT, Appendix 11, para 2(e): <https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/938039/pd-rmit-sep26.pdf> | VERIFIED |

**For slide 13 (Gemini):** sending bill text to Google is a cross-border transfer, and Google is our data processor. Say which section 129 condition a launch would rely on (her explicit consent, or a Transfer Impact Assessment plus Google's data-processing terms). Don't say "PDPA-compliant".

## Don't say

1. "BNM announced the bank measures in 2023." It was **26 Sep 2022**; the kill switch followed in 2023.
2. "BNM requires 24/7 bank scam hotlines." BNM said "dedicated hotlines"; 24/7 is the NSRC.
3. "NSRC is run by NFCC." It is **PDRM CCID** since July 2025.
4. "Banks must reimburse scam victims." SEFT covers only unauthorised transactions; authorised scams (~95%) are excluded.
5. "E-wallets must repay victims within 7 days" as a BNM rule. Only news reports of the PM's July 2026 parliamentary reply.
6. "74,744 scam cases in 2025." Use 66,204 / RM 2.97 billion (IGP) or RM 2.8 billion (BNM).
7. "Seniors lost RM 986 million in 2024" or "elderly cases up 80%." Unverified opinion piece.
8. "Data subjects must be notified within 7 days" under the PDPA. Unverified.
9. "BNM has issued AI rules." Only the Aug 2025 discussion paper exists.
10. "Our Gemini integration is PDPA / RMiT compliant." No basis yet.
