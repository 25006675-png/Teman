# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Ah Por (Mdm Tan, 72):** Cantonese-speaking elder in Malaysia. Weak eyesight, shaky fingers, low trust in banking apps, a main target for scams. Uses her phone to see today's budget, pay bills and ask her assistant questions.
- **Ah Ming (her son, in Singapore):** busy working adult. Opens the Family app when alerted, needs to understand a risky payment in seconds and approve, reject or call Mum.
- **Competition judges and bank experts:** watch the split-screen demo on a projector. They need to see that the AI never authorises a payment and that every decision is explained.

## Product Purpose
An elder-first finance app that pays Grandma's bills in her own dialect and can't be talked into paying a scammer. One AI acts, a second AI checks, fixed rules decide, and family approves. Success: Ah Por pays safe bills alone, scams are stopped, Ah Ming approves risky ones in time, and judges trust the system at a glance.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, warm, modern. Feels like a current banking app (Revolut, Monzo, GXBank), not a form or a dashboard prototype. Calm under pressure; never alarming without saying what to do next.

## Anti-references
- Streamlit / admin-form look: flat bordered boxes, every element the same weight.
- Emoji as the UI icon system.
- Childish "senior mode" with cartoon colours.
- Navy-and-gold "serious bank" cliché.

## Design Principles
1. **One screen, one next action.** Each card makes the next step obvious, with the primary action visually dominant.
2. **Big, never cramped.** Elder text stays large (body ≥ 18px), but hierarchy comes from contrast and space, not boxes.
3. **Status is never colour alone.** Every state has an icon and words, in Cantonese and English.
4. **Show the guardrails.** The engine and bank panels make "no AI approves payments" visible.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard mobile patterns (tab bars, bottom sheets, balance card, list rows) so judges recognise a real app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast minimum, AAA for Ah Por's key amounts. Touch targets ≥ 48px (≥ 56px on Ah Por's primary actions). Hold-to-confirm tolerates tremor. Reduced-motion supported. Cantonese read-aloud on key cards.
